March 2002

Wow!  It’s  been a year since we did this update.  Where does the time go?  Looking back, it’s been an eventful year. Below is a summary.

First of all, as many know, Nichole became a flight attendant.  She flies to Cancun and Punta Canta (Dominican Republic) regularly.  She had 3 weeks of training, 2 in Chicago and was at the top of her class.  Of course, we are all very proud of her!

Cousin Stephanie married Mike in July.  The wedding was fabulous.  You can see the pictures in our picture section.  We spent a week in Texas with the Woodyards and Balfours.  Andrea and Matt were so kind to let us stay at their place.

The boys are great!  They both excel in school.  Nick finally gets traditional letter grades.  His first report card he earned three A’s and two B’s (I’m working on those B’s).  Alex also did well in all his marks (T, O, and P’s).   Their baseball season went well, but they lost in the first round of the playoffs.  Over the summer we joined a pool with a diving board.  Both are excellent swimmers and love to jump off the board.

I got a new job.  The lab I worked for, STL, closed the Baltimore lab.  Now I work for SciTech, a government contractor.  I work at Aberdeen Proving Grounds Edgewood area, 7 miles door-to-door.

My brother George and his wife Nikki had a baby girl, Georgia.  What a head of hair!!!!  You can see pictures from the christening in our picture section.  She will be one in April.

I had my 20 year reunion in September.  Hard to believe it’s been that long…but by all the gray hair I guess there is no denying it.  Pictures are in the WPHS81 section.

Christmas went well again.  The big gift from Santa this year was an X-Box.  Boy, did Santa do good this time!  They both love it.  Mom and Dad like it too!  Mimi and Granddad got the boys a National Football League game for it.  Suddenly they are expert play callers and coaches.

Well, I’m sure I have missed a lot of things but oh well…maybe I’ll be better at this and update monthly or so.
