Hot Chicken Salad

Recipe By : Barbara

Ingredients
2 cups cooked diced chicken
1/2 cup sliced almonds
2 tablespoons grated onion
2 cups coarsely chopped celery
2 tablespoons lemon juice
1/2 tablespoon salt
3/4 cup mayonnaise
1/2 cup grated American or cheddar cheese
1 cup crushed potato chips

Combine first seven ingredients.  Put into flat baking dish and sprinkle with cheese and potato chips.  Bake at 400 degrees for 20 minutes.  Serves 6.
