The Perfect 16 Football Pool

$20 per entry.  Enter as many times as you like.

Objective
Each week you pick one (1) NFL team that you think will win that week. Point spreads are not considered.  If the team you picked wins you pick again the next week.  If they lose or tie you are eliminated from the contest.  You can only pick each team once over the course of the season.  The last remaining undefeated entry(ies) (in terms of weeks) will win the grand prize, 80% of total pool monies.  Second place receives 10%.  To win you need to survive longer than the others, but not necessarily until the end of the season.  If 1 - 5 entry(ies) is eliminated last (i.e.; surviving the whole season or having been eliminated in the same week), those entries will share the grand prize and second place portion equally.  **NEW** If there are 6 or more entries still alive at the end of the regular season, there will be a tie-breaker using the playoffs.  This is done to maximize the winners share (to eliminate splitting the pool 14 ways!).  See Tie-Breaker section below.

Eligible Games
Only National Football League (NFL) games can be picked.  Every scheduled game, starting Week 1 (September 13, 2015) will be available to be chosen except for Thursday and Friday games.  Monday games are considered to be part of the preceding week.  If you pick a game that is postponed, preempted or canceled for any reason, and if that game is not resumed or replayed by the following Wednesday at 7:00AM (EST), you will automatically advance to the next week.

Submitting Picks
Weekly picks can be submitted via PoolHost.com.   Picks for the upcoming week can be submitted starting Tuesday 11AM EST.   Picks can be made/modified up to kickoff of the first scheduled game of that week, either Saturday or Sunday earliest kickoff time ES(D)T, at which time your most recent (closest to the deadline) pick is deemed to be official.  Anyone who does not submit a pick by the weekly deadline or who does so improperly will receive the default pick as explained in the next section.

Default Pick
The default pick is the team who is the largest favorite of the week as determined by the point spreads given by CBS Sportsline (http://www.cbssports.com/nfl/odds/) Friday at noon.  If there is a tie for the largest point spread favorite, the default will be chosen by ascending alphabetical order by city name.   'St. Louis' and not 'Saint Louis' will be used for this purpose.   The default pick must be a team that you have not yet chosen.  If the default pick was already chosen you will receive the next highest favorite, and so on.  If all the favorites have been already chosen (it could happen), you will receive the underdog with the smallest point spread against.  Again, a tie between point spreads here will be resolved through ascending alphabetical order by city name.  The default pick cannot be a Thursday or Friday game.  Note that the default pick may differ from other player's default pick.  Also, there is a three default pick limit for the season.  After you have used 3 default picks, if you fail to get a pick in by the deadline you will be out of the pool.

Buy-back
If you lose in the first four weeks of the pool you can buy back into the pool (in addition to your $20 entry fee).  If you lose week one you can buy-in for $20, if you lose week two you can buy-in for $30, and if you lose week 3 you can buy-in for $40, and if you lose week four you can buy back in for $50.  If you buy back into the pool, your previous picks still count and you cannot pick those teams again.

Stats
On Sunday morning a listing of everyone's picks for the upcoming week will be posted on at PoolHost.com.  Previous picks for everyone will always be available at the same location.  This way you'll know who to cheer for, and there will be no discrepancies as to the number of players who advance.  The Grand Prize will increase continuously as more players join (deadline to join is September 13, 2015).

Tie-Breaker

If there are 6 or more entries remaining at the end of the season, the playoffs will be used as a tie-breaker.  Every game of the playoffs will be used.  Each participant will pick all games of each round.  The participants with the most correct for the round advanced to the next round.  If one participant has more correct than all other, they will win the grand prize (80%) of the pool and the next highest participant(s) will split second place (10%).  If two or more participants have the same number of correct picks, they advance to the next round.  If two or more participants survive to the Super Bowl, then they will pick the Super Bowl winner and total score as a tie-breaker.  The winning pick is the first tie-breaker and closest to the total points scored is the second tie-breaker.  For an example of the tie-breaker, please click here.
